Building a product interface in Blender (prompt buddy)

For this assignment, I will be creating a scene that demonstrates an AI prompting library dashboard tool that I’m going to be redesigning for another class. To get things started, I started by sketching out three displays to showcase the dashboard tool design. Since the dashboard tool was designed for users to copy prompts from the library into an AI tool, the scenes would include two interfaces: a monitor that displays the dashboard design, and another screen that displays an AI tool to imply the dashboard tool’s functionality and usage in action. After evaluation, I decided to go with the first scene as it has the most realistic setup for general employees who use iOS system products. After doing some research, I went with a Blender add-on called “blendkit” and downloaded prebuilt Apple products (monitor, MacBook, keyboard, mouse) and an office desk. I then aligned and rotated objects to make it feel natural. This arrangement would also enable me to render an image that includes both screens in one shot. For next steps, I will be adding an HDRI background and doing research on best practices for adding interfaces to both screens.
